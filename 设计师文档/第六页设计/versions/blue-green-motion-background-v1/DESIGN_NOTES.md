# Eagle Clip — page 06 design brief

- Purpose: explain why ordinary human first-person experience is valuable training material for robots, and make the `2x` research claim feel credible rather than promotional.
- Audience: curious consumers who may wear Eagle Clip, plus technically literate robotics partners.
- Tone: minimal editorial trust — quiet, direct, human, and evidence-led.
- Differentiator: one continuous blue-green motion field runs behind the whole page; the compact square research note and the human conclusion share a single left-right story composition.
- Constraints: preserve every user-provided word verbatim; no final research URL yet; the motion background must never compromise copy contrast; responsive and accessible; no external runtime dependency.

## Local design system

- Palette: deep ocean blue, teal and green motion; white editorial copy; a translucent white research note with black ink.
- Typography: Avenir Next for direct statements; Iowan Old Style for the research note.
- Shape: one compact, nearly square layered research note paired with the closing story; no framed video block.
- Motion: one full-viewport looping background, a restrained paper-lift entrance, subtle pointer tilt, and a two-beat character reveal for the closing story; all motion is disabled under reduced motion.
