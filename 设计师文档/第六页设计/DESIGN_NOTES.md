# Eagle Clip — page 06 two-chapter design brief

- Purpose: explain why ordinary human first-person experience is valuable training material for robots, and make the `2x` research claim feel credible rather than promotional.
- Audience: curious consumers who may wear Eagle Clip, plus technically literate robotics partners.
- Context: a two-screen landing-page chapter — evidence first, human meaning second.
- Tone: minimal editorial trust moving into a warm, lived human moment.
- Differentiator: the information is stacked vertically on a pure white first screen; the second screen becomes a full-bleed real-life photograph while the two closing sentences emerge letter by letter as one story.
- Constraints: preserve every user-provided word verbatim; no final research URL yet; no side-by-side evidence/story layout; exactly two clear vertical chapters; responsive and accessible; no external runtime dependency.

## Local design system

- Palette: pure white and black for evidence; natural sky blue, grass green and black story copy for the lived-experience scene.
- Typography: Avenir Next for direct statements; Iowan Old Style for the research note.
- Spacing: each chapter occupies at least one viewport; page one stacks the title, explanation and compact note on a single vertical axis; page two reserves generous breathing room around the two story beats.
- Shape: one compact layered square research note on page one; no framed image or side-by-side content on page two.
- Motion: a restrained paper-lift entrance, subtle pointer tilt, a slow background-image settle, and a two-beat character reveal for the closing story; all motion is disabled under reduced motion.
- Asset: `assets/images/experience-matters.jpg`, copied locally from the user-provided `素材/背景` image and used as the full-bleed second-screen background.
- Saved alternate: the full-page blue-green motion version is archived under `versions/blue-green-motion-background-v1/` with its local video assets.
