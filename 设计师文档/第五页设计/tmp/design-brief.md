# Eagle Clip evidence page — design brief

- Purpose: answer whether people can really get paid for real-world experience by presenting three concrete company signals.
- Audience: mainstream visitors considering Eagle Clip, including people unfamiliar with embodied AI.
- Context: one immersive website section/page, built in the existing static HTML/CSS/JS project.
- Tone: a warm, sunlit, human-centered future with calm editorial restraint.
- Differentiator: one full-viewport image changes behind a persistent three-company evidence rail, so each claim feels tied to a real robotics scene.
- Content constraint: preserve every user-provided word and number; map Tesla, Figure, and Dyna Robotics to the same-named image files.
- Interaction: click/tap, arrow keys, wheel, swipe, and timed progression; reduced-motion support.
- Responsive behavior: three-column evidence rail on desktop; horizontally snapping company panels with explicit arrow controls on mobile.
- Asset treatment: warm natural light, ivory and honey tones, realistic human activity, a restrained warm-brown bottom gradient, subtle grain, and slow background drift.
