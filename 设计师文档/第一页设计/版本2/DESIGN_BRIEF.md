# Eagle Clip landing page design brief

- Purpose: introduce Eagle Clip as a consumer wearable that turns ordinary first-person experience into useful Physical AI training data.
- Primary audience: everyday contributors first, robotics/data partners second.
- Context: a cinematic, scroll-led product landing page with a real interactive 3D product model.
- Tone: scientific instrument clarity with consumer-level warmth and accessibility.
- Differentiator: one physical Eagle Clip remains pinned in the viewport while scrolling changes its angle, lighting, and meaning—from object, to capture tool, to data bridge.
- Constraints: preserve the supplied STL geometry; make no unsupported hardware or privacy claims; avoid generic neon AI styling; keep the experience usable on mobile and with reduced motion.
- Direction considered: (1) dark scientific instrument, (2) bright consumer utility, (3) field-notebook documentary. Chosen direction combines a dark product stage with a bright trust section, without mixing their typographic systems.
