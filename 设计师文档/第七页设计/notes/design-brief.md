# EgoClip single-page design brief

- Purpose: Introduce EgoClip as a zero-cost wearable that turns ordinary first-person life data into value for embodied AI, and convert interested visitors to an early-access list.
- Context: A single-screen waitlist conversion page with no secondary sections or product walkthrough.
- Tone: Trustworthy, low-saturation sky blue drawn from the supplied lifestyle photography — tactile and human, not cyberpunk or dashboard-like.
- Differentiator: One central email action framed by two real-life EgoClip wearers.
- Constraints: One viewport, existing portraits only, extremely short copy, responsive, keyboard accessible, reduced-motion support.
- System: Muted blue-gray environment, warm ice-glass surfaces, slate ink, Avenir-style rounded typography, 18–24 px controls, only three motion families (entrance, orbit/signal, subtle pointer parallax).
- Revision: Remove the phone, product data overlays, multi-step story and large footer. Keep only the UBL header, two wearer portraits, one value statement and the waitlist form.
- Research notes: Ego4D validates first-person video as a foundation for real-world perception research; Project Aria’s public materials reinforce visible capture status, user control, consent, and privacy as key trust signals for wearables.
