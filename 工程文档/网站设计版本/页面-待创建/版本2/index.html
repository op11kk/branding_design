05 Product principles

Record less like
a black box.

A wearable camera belongs in public life only when its state is understandable and the person wearing it stays in control. These are experience principles for the prototype—not claims about shipped software.

  1. 01
    CAPTURE

    Make state visible.

    The light guide should communicate when the device is active, paused or waiting.

  2. 02
    REVIEW

    See before sharing.

    Moments should return to a calm review surface, not disappear into an invisible pipeline.

  3. 03
    CHOOSE

    Participation is a verb.

    Contribution should be explicit, understandable and reversible by design.

07 From moment to meaning

One view is a moment.
Many can reveal a pattern.

The long-term idea is a permissioned layer made from ordinary life—not a feed of people, not a map of exact addresses, and not a market ticker. The path only matters if people can understand and govern it.
